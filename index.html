A FESTIVAL OF LIGHT, LOVE & LEGACY01 / 04

When the community
comes together.

✳

Navarathri is a time to honour Shakti—the divine feminine energy that inspires strength, wisdom, and renewal.

For nine nights, homes and communities across India celebrate Devi in her many forms. At Aparna Zenon, we welcome our neighbours to share in the prayers, traditions, and togetherness that make this festival so special.
